Who can use String

  • You must be 18 or older. String isn't for anyone under 18, and we remove accounts we believe belong to minors.
  • One account per person, in your own name, with photos of you.
  • You can't use String if we've removed you before, or if the law where you live doesn't allow it.

The String promise

Everyone on String agrees to three things:

  1. A request is an invitation. Anyone can decline, silently. A declined request can't be sent again.
  2. Be who your profile says. One clear photo of you. Fake profiles are removed.
  3. Meet where others are around. Report or block anyone in two taps. We act on every report.

What's not allowed

  • Harassing, threatening, stalking or intimidating anyone, on String or because of something you saw on String.
  • Trying to work out where someone is, for example by moving around to test the radar, combining accounts, or using simulated locations.
  • Impersonating someone, creating fake or multiple accounts, or misrepresenting your age.
  • Sexual content involving minors, nudity, hate speech, violent or illegal content, scams, spam or selling.
  • Collecting other people's data, scraping, automating the app, or getting around its limits or security.
  • Using String for any commercial purpose without our written permission.

We can remove content, pause someone's discovery, or suspend or delete accounts that break these terms. When safety is at stake we may act before reviewing every detail.

Your content

You own what you post: your photos, profile and messages. You give us permission to store it and show it to the people the app shows it to, only to run String. That permission ends when you delete the content or your account, except for report evidence we keep for safety (see the privacy policy).

Meeting people safely

String helps you notice people nearby. It doesn't vet them, and a dot on the radar is never proof of who someone is. Meet in public places with other people around, tell a friend where you are, and leave if anything feels wrong. Report anyone who makes you feel unsafe. If you're in danger, call your local emergency number (112 in India) first.
