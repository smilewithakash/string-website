Your data

Delete your String account

You can delete your String account, and the data that belongs to it, at any time. It takes a few seconds, and it's permanent.

In the app

  1. Open String and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and you're signed out.

Without the app

If you can't open the app any more, email info@string.social from the Google account you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my String account". We'll confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your account and your Google sign-in link with String.
  • Your profile: name, date of birth, bio, interests, intentions, Instagram handle, settings and all your photos (the files too).
  • Your sessions and any current position, your venue room memberships and your personal QR codes.
  • Your requests, connections and chats. The people you were connected with see the chat end.
  • Your blocks, and your phone's notification token.

What is kept

  • Reports. If someone reported you, the report and its evidence (a copy of your profile and recent messages with them) are kept for safety review. If you reported someone, the report stays without your name.
  • Crash reports already sent to Firebase Crashlytics contain no identity and expire there after up to 90 days.
  • Banned accounts only: if our team had banned the account after reviewing reports, we keep a one-way fingerprint of its sign-in ID (not the ID, your email or anything else), so the same account cannot simply sign up again.
  • Server logs (IP address and requested address, no locations or messages) expire after up to 14 days.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep.

Deletion is immediate and final. There's no grace period and it can't be undone. You can sign up again later with the same Google account, but you'll start from scratch.
