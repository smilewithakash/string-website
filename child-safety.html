Child safety standards

Last updated 16 November 2026

String is for adults only. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming and any sexualisation of minors.

Our standards

  • Adults only. Everyone must be 18 or over. We ask for a date of birth at sign-up and refuse anyone under 18. Accounts we believe belong to minors are removed.
  • Prohibited. Any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits or endangers children is forbidden: CSAM, grooming, sextortion, trafficking, and soliciting or arranging contact with minors.
  • No images in chat. Messages are text only. Profile photos are re-encoded on our server, and any photo can be reported.
  • Enforcement. Accounts involved in CSAE are removed immediately and permanently, and the evidence is preserved.
  • Reporting to authorities. We report CSAM and child-endangerment to the relevant authorities, including India's National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and, where applicable, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC). We cooperate with law enforcement.
  • Compliance. We comply with child safety laws that apply to us, including India's Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act and Information Technology Act.

How to report

  1. In the app: open the person's profile or your chat with them, tap ⋯ › Report, and choose a reason. Choose "I feel unsafe" for anything urgent. The report keeps a copy of the evidence, even if they delete the chat.
  2. By email: info@string.social. Don't attach illegal images. Describe what you saw and where.
  3. If a child is in immediate danger, call 112 (India) or your local emergency number first.

Child safety contact

Our designated contact for child safety, including questions from Google Play and authorities, is the String safety team, info@string.social.
