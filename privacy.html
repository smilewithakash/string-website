Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

String helps you meet people who are nearby, without giving away where you are. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and how to delete it. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past.

The short version. You're invisible until you start a session. While you're visible we keep one rounded position (about 30 m) and delete it when the session ends. Nobody ever sees your exact location, distance, direction, surname, phone number or email. We don't sell data, show ads or use advertising or analytics trackers.

Who we are

String is run by the String team in India. For anything about your data, write to info@string.social.

What stays on your phone

Hidden zones (places like home or work where your session stops by itself) are stored and checked only on your phone. We never receive them. Your recent emoji and your crash-report choice stay on your phone too.

Who sees what

  • On the radar and list, people who are visible near you see an anonymous marker, the band you're in (Here now or Nearby), your first name, your age if you allow it, what you're both here for and how many interests you share. Your photo appears on the radar only if you turn on "Show my photo in Nearby". It's off by default.
  • Your profile (photos, bio, interests) can be opened by people who can currently see you on Nearby, in the same venue room, or who scanned your QR code.
  • Your connections also see your Instagram handle, if you added one, and the messages you send them.
  • Nobody ever sees your exact location, distance or direction, your surname, phone number, email address, your hidden zones, or who you've blocked. These rules are enforced on our server, not just hidden in the app.

Location and permissions

String asks for location permission after explaining why, during sign-up (you can say "Not now") or when you first go visible, and only "while using the app". Asking reads no location and sends nothing. String never asks for background ("allow all the time") location. While you're visible a notification is always on screen, with a Stop button. Giving the permission doesn't make you visible: you're visible only during a session you start, and every session ends on its own.

Camera access is used only to scan venue and personal QR codes. Photos you add are picked by you, then re-encoded on our server, which removes hidden data such as the place the photo was taken.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run String. They process data only on our instructions:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in (Authentication), notifications (Cloud Messaging) and crash reports (Crashlytics).
  • Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, in its India West (Mumbai) region, which runs our server and database.
  • An email provider that alerts our safety team when a report comes in. The alert contains only the report's reference, reason and urgency, never names or messages.

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use advertising or analytics SDKs. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it, or when someone's safety is at serious risk.

Security

Data travels encrypted between the app and our server. Photos are served only through short-lived signed links. Screenshots and screen recording are blocked inside the app. Logs never contain locations, tokens or message contents. Access to safety tools is restricted and every action there is logged.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop being visible at any time from the app or the notification.
  • Correct your profile in Profile › Edit profile.
  • Change what others see in Privacy and safety: your age, your photo on the radar, "Show me only as Nearby", who can send you requests, disappearing messages and crash reports.
  • Delete your account in Profile › Account › Delete account. It's immediate and permanent. See Delete your account for details, including how to ask us if you can't open the app.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or about anything in this policy, at info@string.social. We reply within 30 days.

If you're in India, you can also raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer at info@string.social.

Age

String is only for people aged 18 and over. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it. See our child safety standards.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll announce it in the app or on this page before the change applies. The date at the top always shows the latest version.
