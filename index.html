How it works

Three steps. You're in control of every one.

No feeds, no swiping, no strangers sliding into your messages. Just the people in the room, when you want to be found.

STEP 01

Go visible, for as long as you choose

Until you start a session, nobody can see you. Pick 30 minutes, an hour or two, and stop any time from the app or its notification.

STEP 02

See who's here, anonymously

A calm radar shows people within about 300 m who are here for the same thing: networking or friendship. No names on the radar, no exact spots.

STEP 03

Connect only by consent

Send a request with a friendly opener. A chat opens only if they accept. If they don't, nothing happens: declines are silent.

The radar

A radar, not a map.

String tells you someone is close. Never where they are.

  • Two soft bandsHere now (about 50 m) and Nearby (up to 300 m). No distance, no direction.
  • Rounded before anything elsePositions are snapped to a ~30 m grid on our server before they're compared. Exact locations are never stored.
  • Small rooms stay vagueWith fewer than four people around, everyone shows as Nearby, so a quiet room can't give anyone away.
  • Your photo, your callDots are anonymous. Yours shows your photo only if you turn on "Show my photo in Nearby". It's off until you do.

Made for real places

Built for the moment you're both in the room.

Venue rooms

At a meetup, coworking space or event? Join its room with the code on the slide or at the door, and see who else checked in. While you're in a room, String doesn't use your GPS at all.

Your personal QR

Talking to someone already? Let them scan your code. It refreshes constantly, so a screenshot of it won't work later, and it opens your profile preview. It never connects you by itself.

Requests with good manners

Choose from ready-made openers, up to three requests a day. Requests expire after 24 hours, and a decline is never announced. Free text starts only after you're both in.

Chats that can disappear

Turn on disappearing messages and each side's view clears when they leave the chat. Screenshots are blocked across the app, and "Cut the string" ends a connection and its chat for both of you.

Privacy

Privacy isn't a setting. It's the design.

Every rule below is enforced on our server, not just hidden in the app.

Invisible by default

Nobody sees you, and no location leaves your phone, until you start a session.

No location history

We keep one rounded position while you're visible and delete it the moment your session ends.

Hidden zones

Mark places like home or work. Step into one and your session stops. The check runs on your phone, so those places never reach us.

Always time-boxed

Every session ends on its own. Auto-renew is off unless you switch it on, and it stops after 12 hours.

Stop from anywhere

A visible notification runs while you're discoverable. One tap on Stop ends it immediately.

Quiet notifications

Lock-screen notifications never name anyone or show a message. No "allow all the time" location, ever.

Safety

Calm, intentional and trustworthy.

Meeting people nearby only works if everyone feels safe doing it.

Block, completely

Blocked people vanish for each other everywhere: radar, lists, venue rooms, QR and chat. They're never told.

Report, with evidence

A report keeps a copy of what happened, even if they delete the chat. "I feel unsafe" is treated as urgent, and repeated reports pause someone's discovery until we review it.

Adults only

String is for people 18 and over. Dating stays switched off until we can verify identities. For now it's networking and friendship.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is String a dating app?
Not right now. String launches for networking and friendship. Dating stays switched off until we can verify identities properly.
Can people see exactly where I am?
No. Others only see that you're Here now (about 50 m) or Nearby (up to 300 m), and only while you're visible. Never a distance, a direction or a point on a map. Positions are rounded to a ~30 m grid on our server, and with fewer than four people around everyone shows as Nearby.
Does String track me in the background?
Only during a session you start, with an ongoing notification so you always know. String never asks for "Allow all the time" location. When your session ends, its last position is deleted. In a venue room String doesn't use GPS at all.
Who can message me?
Only people whose request you accept. Requests use preset openers, there's a limit of three a day, they expire after 24 hours, and you can choose to accept requests only from people who share an interest with you.
What happens when I block someone?
You disappear for each other everywhere in String: radar, list, venue rooms, QR and chat. Any connection and chat between you ends. They're not told.
How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to Profile › Account › Delete account. It's immediate and permanent. See Delete your account for what's removed.
When can I get it?
String is launching on Android with a few pilot events first. iPhone comes later. Running an event? Talk to us.
